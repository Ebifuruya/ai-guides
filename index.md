# AI Prompting Guides

Practical, field-tested techniques for getting better results from AI assistants. No programming knowledge required.

---

## The Guides

### [Keeping Your AI Sharp v5.0](keeping-your-ai-sharp)

Why AI answers lose quality over the course of a conversation — and ten techniques to prevent it. Covers context-window degradation, thinking-time limits, and practical fixes ranging from thread management to prompt editing.

### [Prompt Directives Guide v8.3](prompt-directives-guide)

A structured system for writing better LLM prompts using labeled directives (Goal, Role, Context, Task, Audience, Tone, Format, Constraints). Includes scoping rules, nesting conventions, and full worked examples.

---

*Both guides work with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other major AI assistants.*
